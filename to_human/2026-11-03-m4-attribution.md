# Progress report — 2026-11-03 (M4 attribution complete)

**Short version.** The attribution experiment is done and the answer is negative — and that is the most valuable result so far. A physics-informed forecaster (kinematics in the loss) and an identical physics-free twin were trained across four data regimes on ROAD. Result: **parity everywhere** (AP 0.71 vs 0.71; the twin even edges ahead at 0.1% training data), confirmed robust across a lambda sweep of 0.1-10. Mechanism: the "physics" here is a linear display-wheel relation that the twin learns from ~1,000 frames, and 1M ambient frames make soft constraints redundant. This reproduces the Grossmann/Krishnapriyan lesson in CPS security: physics priors pay off when the physics is hard to learn or data is scarce — neither holds here.

**What the paper now claims (honest, ablation-backed):** (1) the physics *taxonomy* of ROAD attacks (which law each class violates); (2) analytical physics residuals detect 3/4 masquerade classes with zero attack data; (3) learned soft-physics adds nothing over an equal-capacity twin on this dataset — with the mechanism and the lambda-robustness shown; (4) the reverse-light class evades all value/physics detectors (dynamically consistent snapshot) — a boundary finding; (5) a reusable evaluation protocol.

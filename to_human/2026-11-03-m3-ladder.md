# Progress report — 2026-11-03 (M3 detector ladder complete)

**Short version.** The full baseline ladder is implemented and evaluated with ambient-only calibration and capture-disjoint supervised splits. Physics residuals and supervised ML (RF/XGBoost) both catch the wheel-speed, speedometer, and coolant masquerade classes perfectly. Two findings matter for the paper:

1. **The reverse-light masquerade evades physics AND supervised ML** (best AP 0.49/0.63) — the injector live-patches the light bit into a dynamically consistent snapshot, and ROAD's anonymized DBC hides the gear signal needed for a semantic law. Honest boundary of kinematic physics.
2. **A one-class autoencoder that never saw attacks ranks that same class at AP 0.975** (though not at a strict operating point) — while XGBoost collapses on the coolant class where only 156 attack frames exist for training and physics needed none.

Where the "physics contribution" now genuinely lives: no-attack-data requirements, small-sample robustness, and false-positive behavior on benign stress — which is exactly what M4's PINN-vs-physics-free-twin attribution experiment measures next.
